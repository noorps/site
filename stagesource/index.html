From script to load-in in three steps

  1. 1

    Upload the script

    Paste it, drop a text file, or add your production notes. StageSource reads the stage directions and dialogue for everything the show needs.

  2. 2

    Get your sourcing checklist

    Props, furniture, costumes, lighting and effects, with quantities, the scenes they appear in, and the details that matter (period, color, "must hold liquid").

  3. 3

    Borrow it from down the street

    Each item is matched against inventory from theaters, schools and suppliers near you. Request it, arrange pickup, return it after strike.

Why we're building it

Less ends up in a dumpster

Most sets, props and costumes are built or bought for one run, then stored, then thrown out. Every rental is one less item made new and one less thrown away after strike.

More of the budget goes to the work

Small companies spend weeks of volunteer time and a real share of their budget hunting down things that already exist a few miles away. That time belongs to rehearsal, and that money belongs to artists.

Storage rooms start paying their way

A costume room or scene shop full of past seasons is income waiting to happen. Providers set their own rates, or lend for free to the groups they want to support.
