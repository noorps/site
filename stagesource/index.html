Cue 04

Why we're building it.

  1. 1

    Less ends up in a dumpster.

    Most sets, props and costumes are made for one run, stored, then thrown out. Every rental is one less thing built new and one less thing binned after strike.

  2. 2

    More of the budget goes to artists.

    Volunteer hours spent driving around for a rotary phone belong in rehearsal. Money spent buying one belongs to the people on stage.

  3. 3

    Storage rooms start paying rent.

    A costume room full of past seasons is income waiting to happen. Providers set their own rates, or lend free to the groups they want to back.
